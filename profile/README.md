# PropSim: prop firm software

**[PropSim](https://propsim.markets/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=org_profile&utm_campaign=propsim_enterprise) is white-label software for starting and running a prop trading firm.** Sell trading challenges on your own brand, evaluate traders with a risk engine that checks every tick against your rules, give them a trader terminal (or connect your own MT4 or MT5 server), and pay out funded traders, all from one back office. You can start on the free plan.
